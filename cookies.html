01. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences, improve functionality and understand how visitors interact with pages.

02. HOW WE USE COOKIES

VEXONIQ STUDIO may use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential website functionality.
  • Remembering user preferences.
  • Understanding website traffic and performance.
  • Improving the overall user experience.
  • Advertising and measuring ad performance, where enabled.

03. TYPES OF COOKIES

Essential Cookies

These support basic website functions and security. They cannot be disabled through our website controls where they are necessary for operation.

Analytics Cookies

These may help us understand visitor activity and improve website performance.

Advertising Cookies

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, cookies may be used to deliver, personalize or measure ads according to applicable consent and privacy requirements.
